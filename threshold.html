Never in your app

  • AUROC described as “accuracy”.
  • A training result labelled as a test result.
  • A causal claim about a feature.

Ideas, not templates Optional

  • Two strips of dots, one for adults with diabetes and one without, with the threshold as a line you drag.
  • Say the trade in the lead's units: “per 100 adults screened, N more follow-up tests to find M more cases”, with N and M computed by m11.
  • For readmission, there is no fixed target: show what each threshold gains and gives up, and let the lead choose.

Accurate, readable tables meet every required minimum. Custom visuals are optional.

Common questions

I set the threshold while looking at the test patients. Is that a problem?

A little. A threshold chosen to reach 0.90 sensitivity on these test patients will usually do slightly worse on new ones (the lecture calls this peeking ?). In a real project you would set it on separate data. Here, treat your test sensitivity as likely optimistic.

Why doesn't AUROC change when I move the slider?

AUROC describes how the model ranks patients across every threshold at once. Moving the threshold changes who is flagged, not the ranking.

Should the slider go in steps of 0.01?

That's fine. Steps of 0.01 are fine enough to reach 0.90 sensitivity for every model.
