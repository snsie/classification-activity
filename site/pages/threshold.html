Common questions

I set the threshold while looking at the test patients. Is that a problem?

A little. A threshold chosen to reach 0.90 sensitivity on these test patients will usually do slightly worse on new ones (lecture §15 calls this peeking {{help peeking}}). In a real project you would set it on separate data. Here, say in Canvas that your test sensitivity is likely optimistic.

Why doesn't AUROC change when I move the slider?

AUROC describes how the model ranks patients across every threshold at once. Moving the threshold changes who is flagged, not the ranking.

Should the slider go in steps of 0.01?

That's fine. Steps of 0.01 are fine enough to reach 0.90 sensitivity for every model.
