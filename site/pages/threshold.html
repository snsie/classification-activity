A Threshold for the Job

What does reaching the job's sensitivity target cost?

Learning objective
After using it, the clinical lead can see that the threshold {{help threshold}} is a decision separate from the model, and what the job's rule gains and gives up compared with 0.50.
The job's rule
Diabetes: among development out-of-fold thresholds where sensitivity is at least 0.90, choose the one with the highest specificity. {{help diabetes-rule}}
Data
Development out-of-fold probabilities {{help oof}}. The rule is never applied to the test set {{help peeking}}.

Required minimum Required

  • {{help threshold-for-the-job}}

Done when

Reviewers will try

Diabetes: what does reaching 0.90 sensitivity cost in false alarms per 100 adults?

Never in your app

  • AUROC described as “accuracy”.
  • An SVM score described as a probability.
  • A CV result labelled as a test result.
  • A causal claim about a feature.

Ideas, not templates Optional

  • Two strips of dots, one for adults with diabetes and one without, with the threshold as a line you drag.
  • Say the trade in the lead's units: “per 100 adults screened, N more follow-up tests to find M more cases”, with N and M computed by m11.
  • For readmission and heart, there is no fixed rule: show what each threshold gains and gives up, and let the lead choose.

Accurate, readable tables meet every required minimum. Custom visuals are optional.

Where your numbers come from

  • m11.confusion(p, y, t) and m11.metrics(...) at the slider's value.
  • m11.thresholdForRule(p, y) / m11.threshold_for_rule(p, y): the rule's threshold for any model (development only).
  • results/test_record.json → diabetes_threshold: your saved model, threshold and rule. m11.load("test_record") returns null before you run the test.
  • After the test: m11.testPreds(test, model) gives the test probabilities, if you show how the saved threshold did on new adults (label it Test).

Common questions

Before I run the test, there is no saved threshold. What do I mark?

Show the rule's threshold for the selected model, labelled “not saved yet”. Once test_record.json exists, mark the saved one.

Why not set the threshold on the test set, where I can see it reach 0.90?

That is peeking (lecture §15): set on the test patients, a threshold always reaches its target there, and promises more than new patients will get.

Should the slider snap to the saved threshold?

It can. A 0.01-step slider may not land exactly on it, so show the saved threshold's numbers in their own column.
