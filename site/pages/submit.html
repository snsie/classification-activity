Canvas reasoning45Own numbers, correct interpretation, tied to the job, uncertainty stated (15 each)

No points for higher AUROC, for choosing a particular model or for visual polish. The design-choice points reward a deliberate, explained decision, not a good-looking app: “I put flagged adults per 100 first because the clinical lead staffs the clinic” earns full credit in a plain app.

Class gallery Ungraded

After the deadline, every app goes into a class gallery. Open a few of your classmates' apps and vote in three categories:

  • The app a clinical lead would actually open: the reader could make the decision from it, cold.
  • Most honest about uncertainty: it shows what the model can't do, not only what it can.
  • Clearest threshold trade-off: it makes plain what moving the threshold gains, and who carries the cost.

Votes don't change your grade. The winners are shown to the class, with their design choices.

False claims to avoid

None of these may appear anywhere in your app:

  • AUROC described as “accuracy” {{help auroc}}
  • a training result labelled as a test result {{help training-auroc}}
  • a causal claim about a feature
