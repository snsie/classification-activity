A Fair Comparison

Is the best-looking model really better, or just lucky on these folds?

Learning objective
After using it, the clinical lead can judge whether one model is really better than another, and why the test set {{help test-set}} is opened only once.
The rule
Lecture §16: when a model's lead over logistic regression is within one SD {{help within-one-sd}} of the fold-to-fold spread, prefer the simpler model. Simplest first: logreg, kNN, SVM, random forest, gradient boosting.
Data
Training and CV AUROC per fold {{help cv-auroc}} from development, and test AUROC after the test run.

Required minimum Required

  • {{help training-auroc}} {{help fold-sd}}

Done when

Reviewers will try

Heart: is the best model's lead over logreg bigger than one fold SD? Does the test result fall inside the CV range?

Never in your app

  • AUROC described as “accuracy”.
  • An SVM score described as a probability.
  • A CV result labelled as a test result.
  • A causal claim about a feature.

Ideas, not templates Optional

  • One row per model: five grey dots for training, five coloured dots for CV, and a bar for mean ± SD.
  • Shade logreg's ± 1 SD band behind every row, so a lead inside it is easy to see.
  • Before the test run, show the test as a locked box: what will open, and when.

Accurate, readable tables meet every required minimum. Custom visuals are optional.

Where your numbers come from

  • m11.foldSummary(dev) / m11.fold_summary(dev): for each model, train_folds, cv_folds, mean_train, sd_train, mean_cv, sd_cv, lead_over_logreg and within_one_sd; plus best and favoured.
  • results/<key>_test.json: each model's test auroc, with n and n_pos for the denominators. m11.load returns null until you run the test.

Common questions

Which SD does “within one SD of logreg” use?

Logistic regression's fold-to-fold SD of CV AUROC, as in lecture §16. The lead is the model's mean CV AUROC minus logreg's.

Heart's test result is higher than CV. Did something go wrong?

Not necessarily. Heart has only {{num heart.n_test}} test patients, so one test AUROC can land well away from the CV mean. That noise is the point of the heart dataset.

Can I rerun the test after I change something?

Only with --force, which is logged. Once you've looked at the test set and changed something, it is no longer unseen (lecture §13). Explain surprises in Canvas instead.
