Common questions

The random forest's training AUROC is almost 1, and gradient boosting's is not far behind. Is either the best model?

No. Their trees can memorise the training patients, so they rank them almost perfectly. That says little about new patients. Judge every model by its test AUROC.

One model's test AUROC is a little higher than its training AUROC. Did something go wrong?

Not necessarily. A simple model like logistic regression barely overfits, and with a test set of about a thousand patients, its test AUROC can land a little above or below its training AUROC by chance.

Why not keep trying models until the test AUROC is highest?

Every choice made by looking at the test set makes its score more optimistic. The models here are fixed, so the test set stays a fair check.
