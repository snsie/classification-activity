Ranking vs. Being Right

Can a model that ranks patients well still give the wrong risk?

Learning objective
After using it, the clinical lead can read an ROC curve {{help roc-curve}}, say what AUROC {{help auroc}} does and doesn't mean, and tell good ranking apart from trustworthy probabilities {{help calibration-plot}}.
Data
Development out-of-fold probabilities {{help oof}}, every threshold at once.

Required minimum Required

Done when

Reviewers will try

Find two models with similar AUROC but different calibration. Which would you let tell a patient “your risk is 30%”?

Never in your app

  • AUROC described as “accuracy”.
  • An SVM score described as a probability.
  • A CV result labelled as a test result.
  • A causal claim about a feature.

Ideas, not templates Optional

  • Draw a random pair: one patient with the outcome, one without. Which got the higher probability? AUROC is the share of pairs the model gets right.
  • Put two models' calibration plots side by side, on the same axes.
  • Mark the 0.50 point on each ROC curve, so the lead sees where Section 2's matrix sits.

Accurate, readable tables meet every required minimum. Custom visuals are optional.

Where your numbers come from

  • m11.roc(p, y): fpr, tpr, thr (one point per distinct threshold) and auroc.
  • m11.calibration(p, y): 10 equal-size groups, each with mean_pred, observed and n.
  • Use development out-of-fold probabilities, pooled across the 5 folds. Section 4 shows the per-fold AUROCs.

Common questions

The SVM's probabilities look fine. Wasn't its score not a probability?

Its raw score isn't. The supplied helper converted it before anything was exported, so the SVM's results hold probabilities. Judge them with the calibration plot, like any other model's.

Why is the calibration plot's axis so short for readmission?

Few discharges get a high probability. Scale the axes to the data, and keep the diagonal (predicted = observed) visible.

Is an AUROC of 0.80 good?

There's no universal cut-off (lecture §9). It depends on the job and the alternatives: modest ranking can help prioritise phone calls and still be inadequate for a diagnosis.
