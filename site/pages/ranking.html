  • A calibration plot (10 groups) for the selected model, to show whether a predicted 30% really means about 30 in 100.
  • Draw a random pair: one patient with the outcome, one without. Which got the higher probability? AUROC is the share of pairs the model gets right.

Common questions

Why is the calibration plot's axis so short for readmission?

Few discharges get a high probability. Scale the axes to the data, and keep the diagonal (predicted = observed) visible.

Is an AUROC of 0.80 good?

There's no universal cut-off. It depends on the job and the alternatives: modest ranking can help prioritise phone calls and still be inadequate for a diagnosis.
