App section 3

Training vs. Test

Does the model do as well on new patients as on the ones it learned from?

Learning objective
After using it, the clinical lead can see why a model is judged on patients it never saw ?, and spot a model that memorised its training patients ?.
Data
Each model's training AUROC ? and test AUROC.

Required minimum Required

Ask your agent

Build Section 3, Training vs. Test, to this required minimum: <paste the list above>. Take the AUROCs and gaps from m11.auroc_summary and the patient counts from n in the results' train and test. Offer me two or three layouts first; build the one I choose.

Done when

Reviewers will try

Readmission: which model looks best on its training patients, and does it stay best on the test patients?

Never in your app

  • AUROC described as “accuracy”.
  • A training result labelled as a test result.
  • A causal claim about a feature.

Ideas, not templates Optional

  • One row per model: a grey dot for training AUROC, a coloured dot for test AUROC, and a line between them, so the gap is something you can see.
  • Sort the models by test AUROC, not training AUROC, and say why.
  • Say the gap in plain words: “on patients it had already seen, it ranked almost perfectly; on new patients, it ranked like the others”.

Accurate, readable tables meet every required minimum. Custom visuals are optional.

Common questions

The random forest's training AUROC is almost 1, and gradient boosting's is not far behind. Is either the best model?

No. Their trees can memorise the training patients, so they rank them almost perfectly. That says little about new patients. Judge every model by its test AUROC.

One model's test AUROC is a little higher than its training AUROC. Did something go wrong?

Not necessarily. A simple model like logistic regression barely overfits, and with a test set of about a thousand patients, its test AUROC can land a little above or below its training AUROC by chance.

Why not keep trying models until the test AUROC is highest?

Every choice made by looking at the test set makes its score more optimistic. The models here are fixed, so the test set stays a fair check.
