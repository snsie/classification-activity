The two datasets

Each CSV has the same layout: row_id; split (train or test; 80/20, stratified, seed 0); the predictors; and target (1 = the positive class). Blank cells are missing values.

  • Use the split column. Every model learns from the same training rows and is scored on the same test rows; never reshuffle.
  • Check “When is it known?” for every column ?. A predictor must exist at the prediction point.
  • One row per person in every dataset, so each person's records are already kept together ?.

30-day readmission

readmission.csv 2.5 MB

Job
Predict readmission within 30 days, at discharge.
Positive class
Readmitted within 30 days (target = 1) ?
Prediction point
At discharge
Cohort
UCI Diabetes 130-US Hospitals 1999–2008: inpatient stays of patients with diabetes. First discharge per patient, excluding discharge to hospice or death, then a stratified random sample of 20,000 (random_state=0).
Framing
Results apply to this historical inpatient cohort involving diabetes, not to all discharges.

From the supplied data 20,000 discharges, 1,796 positive (9.0%). Training: 16,000 (1,437 positive). Test: 4,000 (359 positive).

Diabetes screening

diabetes.csv 348 KB

Job
Flag adults for follow-up testing; finding positives matters most.
Positive class
Diabetes (reported doctor diagnosis or HbA1c ≥ 6.5%) (target = 1) ?
Prediction point
At the survey examination, before the blood test results
Cohort
NHANES 2017–2018: adults (18+) with an HbA1c measurement who answered “Has a doctor ever told you that you have diabetes?” with yes, no or borderline.
Framing
The label is “reported doctor diagnosis or HbA1c ≥ 6.5%”. It includes adults who were already diagnosed. It identifies the label at one time point and does not predict future diabetes. Results describe adults in this sample.

From the supplied data 5,257 adults, 997 positive (19.0%). Training: 4,205 (797 positive). Test: 1,052 (200 positive).

Using the starter

The starter has the same plain app twice; you build one of them. Either way it reads results/ through the metric helpers. Your agent reads AGENT_BRIEF.md 10 KB first: the starting prompt tells it to. The starter has no analysis/models.py or run_app.sh: your agent writes them.
